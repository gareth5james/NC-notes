# Our Code of Ethics

Northcoders is a place where everybody is welcomed, respected and included. We expect everyone in our community to follow our Code of Ethics. 

We enforce this Code rigorously, and if anyone seriously breaches the Code, be in no doubt that we will remove them from the course. 

## As members of the Northcoders community, we all collectively and also each of us individually agree to adhere to the following principles and commitments: 


### Are welcoming and inclusive 

We welcome and support people regardless of background or identity.

**This includes, but is not limited to, members of any race, ethnicity, culture, national origin, colour, immigration status, social and economic class, educational level, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, age, size, family status, political belief, religion, and mental and physical ability.**

We don’t engage in any demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behaviour or speech and any breach of this principle by you shall constitute a breach of this Code of Ethics which may lead to your suspension and/or expulsion from the Course.


### Treat one another with respect 

We treat others, student or team member, respectfully. We do this even if we disagree on technical or social matters.

Northcoders is a place for learning. So we support each other with patience and compassion. We resolve disagreements and differing views constructively. We are all prone to becoming frustrated during the course, but we won’t allow frustration to turn into personal attacks. 

Any breach of this principle by you shall constitute a breach of this Code of Ethics which may lead to your suspension and/or expulsion from the Course.


### Are considerate of others 

We are considerate of other members of our community, particularly their personal boundaries, and are aware that decisions we take can affect other people.

Any breach of this principle by you shall constitute a breach of this Code of Ethics which may lead to your suspension and/or expulsion from the Course.


### Respect the space in which we work

We acknowledge that we share our space with many other people as well as other companies, and will behave respectfully and appropriately to others, accordingly. 

This means but is not limited to respecting our shared environment, keeping our work areas tidy, refraining from inappropriate language and extending the above behaviours to all members of the community. 

Any breach of this principle by you shall constitute a breach of this Code of Ethics which may lead to your suspension and/or expulsion from the Course. 

Definitions: Harassing Behaviour 

Offensive, unwelcome or demeaning: anything that - whether intentionally or not - will cause someone discomfort, upset, annoyance or perceived loss of respect or dignity 

Harassment includes but is not limited to: 
- Offensive, unwelcome or demeaning comments related to any of the backgrounds and identities outlined previously
- Offensive, unwelcome or demeaning comments regarding a person’s lifestyle choices and practices, including those related to physical appearance, food, health, parenting, drugs, and employment 
- Deliberate misgendering. This includes deadnaming or persistently using a pronoun that does not correctly reflect a person’s gender identity. You must address people by the name they give you when not addressing them by their username or handle 
- Physical contact and simulated physical contact (eg, textual descriptions like “hug” or “backrub”) without consent or after a request to stop 
- Threats of violence, both physical and psychological 
- Incitement of violence towards any individual, including encouraging a person to commit suicide or to engage in self-harm 
- Deliberate intimidation 
- Stalking or following 
- Harassing photography or recording, including logging online activity for harassment purposes 
- Sustained disruption of discussion 
- Unwelcome sexual attention, including gratuitous sexual comments, images or behaviour 
- Pattern of inappropriate social contact, such as requesting/assuming inappropriate levels of intimacy with others 
- Continued one-on-one communication after requests to cease 
- Deliberate “outing” of any aspect of a person’s identity without their consent except as necessary to protect others from intentional abuse 
- Publication of private communication 

Any student found to have harassed another student, member of the support staff or faculty or member of the wider public shall be deemed to have breached this principle and the Code of Ethics which breach may lead to that student’s suspension and/or expulsion from the Course. 

## Reporting Issues

We hope that all members of our community feel able to speak to us at any time about any concerns they may have about another person’s conduct. Reports of any concerning behaviour should be made to your mentor, our Heads of Mentoring (Paul Rogerson and Vel Georgieva) or anyone you feel comfortable speaking to at Northcoders. We’ll deal with any information sensitively. 

We’ll ask you to include names of anyone involved, an account of what occurred, and any other helpful information such as names of others who may have observed the event. 

If for any reason you would rather send us your report of any concerns to us in writing than speak to us directly, please use this short questionnaire to report the incident to us: https://northcoders.typeform.com/to/w3bHts

When we receive a report, it will be treated with discretion and we will work to determine what happened, whether this constitutes a breach of the Code of Ethics, and how to proceed. After we have reached a decision we may request a personal or public apology, a private reprimand, or either a temporary or permanent exclusion from Northcoders. 

In the event that you have been determined to have breached this Code of Ethics, you may appeal the determination by submitting your grounds for appeal in writing within 5 days of the date of issue of our determination, appeals will be heard by the chairperson of the appeal process, the Chief Operating Officer ([coo@northcoders.com](coo@northcoders.com)) within 3 days of receipt of your appeal. The decision of the chairperson of the appeal process will be final and binding.
